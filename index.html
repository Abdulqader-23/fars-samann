Who he is

Biography

Fars Samann was born in Baghdad, Iraq, in 1988. He received his B.Sc. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Duhok, Kurdistan Region of Iraq, in 2011, and his M.Sc. in Electronic Communication and Computer Engineering from the University of Nottingham, United Kingdom.

He joined the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Duhok in 2014 as an assistant lecturer, and moved to the Department of Biomedical Engineering in 2019. He completed his Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering at the Faculty of Life Science Engineering (LSE), Technische Hochschule Mittelhessen (THM), Gießen, Germany, with the highest distinction, Summa Cum Laude.

In 2026 he became Assistant Professor in Signal Processing and Systems at the University of Duhok. He collaborates actively with researchers across electrical, computer and biomedical engineering.

What he works on

Research

His work teaches computers to clean and understand signals from the human body. Heart (ECG), muscle (EMG), pulse (PPG) and brain recordings are often hidden under noise. He designs neural networks and sparse models that remove this noise, so the real signal becomes clear enough for diagnosis, even on small wearable devices.

Cleaning heart signals

Denoising autoencoders such as RunDAE and ML-CDAE for single-lead and 12-lead ECG.

Real-time and wearable

Small, sparse and shallow networks fast enough to run in real time.

Beyond the heart

Muscle fatigue from EMG, PPG separation and neural spike sorting.

Research interests

  • Biomedical engineering
  • Biomedical signal processing
  • Denoising bio-signals with denoising autoencoders
  • Bio-signal processing with neural networks
  • Sparse modelling of biomedical signals
  • ECG, EMG, PPG and EEG analysis
